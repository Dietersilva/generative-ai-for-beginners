A Trip to the Moon (1902)

Méliès gets this effect with nothing but a papier-mâché moon and his signature stop-substitution splice — a trick he stumbled into by accident years earlier when his camera jammed mid-shot on a Paris street. It's the first special-effects image anyone still remembers, reproduced for over a century, from posters to a Smashing Pumpkins video.
