Romero shot this on a shoestring in rural Pennsylvania, and the grainy 16mm newsreel look wasn't a style choice — it's just what a $114,000 budget gets you. That accidental realism is what made it terrifying. It also invented the modern zombie outright; the word "zombie" never appears in the script.
