Nosferatu (1922)

← Home

This single pose — Schreck's silhouette, fingers splayed into claws against the window light — is the most reproduced image from the entire film, used on posters and in textbooks ever since. Murnau builds to it with almost a minute of unbroken tension, then holds the shape just long enough to burn it into memory.
