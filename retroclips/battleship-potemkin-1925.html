Battleship Potemkin (1925)

← Retour

The massacre on the steps never actually happened -- Eisenstein invented it for the film -- but the sequence's editing rhythm is so influential it's taught in nearly every film school on earth, and its unattended-carriage-rolling-downstairs image has been directly restaged in everything from The Untouchables to Naked Gun 33 1/3. Montage editing as a technique essentially starts here.
