Tumbleweeds (1925)

← Retour

Tumbleweeds restages the actual 1893 Cherokee Strip land rush with a cast of hundreds, and it was William S. Hart's final film after a career as one of the silent Western's biggest stars. A 1939 re-release added an eight-minute spoken prologue, the only time Hart's voice was ever captured on film, reflecting on what he called "the glories of the old west." The site's other Westerns cover the wagon trail (The Covered Wagon) and the railroad (The Iron Horse); this fills in the land rush.
