The Cameraman's Revenge (1912)

← Retour

Ladislas Starevich made this entirely with dead insects rigged into articulated stop-motion puppets, reportedly a workaround after his live beetles refused to perform under studio lights. The technique was so far ahead of anything else being attempted with animation that some contemporary audiences assumed the insects had been trained rather than animated frame by frame, and Starevich never fully explained his methods. It stands as one of the earliest and strangest examples of stop-motion character animation, made in Moscow a full generation before King Kong or the Rankin-Bass holiday specials it quietly anticipates.
