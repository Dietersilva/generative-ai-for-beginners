Six seconds of film history. Twenty seconds of why it matters.

Do you love classic movies? So do we — we built this hoping you'll fall for one you've never seen, one shot at a time.

Every clip on this site is sourced from a film whose US copyright has expired or lapsed — not a fair-use claim on a current release.

Prototype build. All six clips are real footage sourced from archive.org — see README.md for sourcing details and how to add more.
