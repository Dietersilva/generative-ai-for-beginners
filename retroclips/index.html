Six seconds of film history. Twenty seconds of why it matters.

Do you love classic movies? So do we — we built this hoping you'll fall for one you've never seen, one shot at a time. A lot of early film history is underseen, under-preserved, or just plain forgotten, and we don't think it should be — this is our small effort to help keep it in circulation.
