Afgrunden (1910)

← Retour

Afgrunden made Asta Nielsen an overnight international star on the strength of a naturalistic, physically committed acting style that had no real precedent in film at the time. This dance number was considered scandalous enough on release that the film was censored in both Norway and Sweden. Nielsen's performance here is widely cited by historians as an influence on the American screen vamp archetype that Theda Bara would make famous five years later in A Fool There Was, already on this site.
