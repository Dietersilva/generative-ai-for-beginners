Pick a Grand Prix to replay. Greyed rounds aren't in this replay yet.

Timing, car positions and team radio come from OpenF1, an unofficial community API. Car positions are sampled about 4 times a second, so the cars are smoothed between samples. Crashes are inferred from the telemetry (a retiring car that drops from racing speed to a standstill), not from an official feed. This is a private fan project with no connection to Formula 1 or the FIA.
